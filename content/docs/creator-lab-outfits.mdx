title: Dress the ped
description: Choose a ped, put clothes on and take them off, pick items and colours, and move around the stage.
---

Everything on this page happens in the left column and on the stage. Nothing is
saved until you press **Capture view** or **Capture range**, so try anything.

## Choose the ped

The **Ped** switch at the top of the left column swaps between the male and
female ped.

<Callout type="warn" title="Switching starts a fresh outfit">
  The male and female peds have their own clothes, numbered separately: male
  top 12 and female top 12 are different items. Switching the ped resets the
  outfit to the plain starting one.
</Callout>

## The outfit list

Each row in **Outfit** is one clothing slot:

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Tops / Jackets | Shirts, jackets, hoodies: the outer layer |
| Undershirts | What shows under an open jacket |
| Torsos | The arms and hands. Pair this with the top (see below) |
| Legs | Trousers, shorts, skirts |
| Shoes | Footwear |
| Masks | Masks and face coverings |
| Hair | Hairstyles |
| Accessories | Chains, ties, scarves |
| Armor | Vests |
| Bags | Backpacks and parachutes |
| Decals | Badges and logos printed on clothes |

A row with nothing worn shows a **Wear** button. Press it to put item 0 of that
slot on the ped. A worn row shows two counters and an **×** that takes the item
off again.

### The two counters

- The **left counter** picks the item. Use **−** and **+**, or click the number
  and type one.
- The **right counter** picks the colour or pattern of that item.
- The line underneath shows how many items the slot has and how many colours the
  current item has, for example `of 628 drawables, 16 textures`. "Drawable" is
  the game's word for an item and "texture" its word for a colour. They are the
  same numbers the Directory and the picture addresses use.

When you change the item, the colour goes back to 0, because every item has its
own number of colours. You cannot go past the last item or colour; the counter
stops there.

<Callout title="Arms look wrong?">
  In GTA the arms and hands are a separate part (Torsos) that has to match the
  top. If arms poke through a jacket, or sleeves show bare skin where there
  should be cloth, change the **Torsos** number until it fits. Short sleeves,
  long sleeves and gloves each need a different torso.
</Callout>

### Empty items

A few numbers in some slots are empty in the game itself: invisible
placeholders such as "no top" or "no mask". Picking one shows nothing on the
ped. That is the game, not a bug. Step past it with **+**.

## Move around the stage

- **Drag** on the stage to turn the ped.
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out.

This only changes your view while you look around. Pictures use the **Angle**
and **Framing** settings under the stage, so you get the same shot every time.
See [Frame and save a picture](/docs/creator-lab-pictures).

## Open an item straight from the Directory

Every item in the [Directory](/browse) has a **Try it on** link. It opens the
Creator Lab with:

- the right ped already chosen,
- that item and colour on the ped,
- **Only the garment** pointed at that slot,
- and **Batch capture** set to that item.

You can build the same link yourself. The address takes the slot, item, colour
and ped:

```txt
/creator?slot=tops&d=245&t=0&g=male
```

`d` is the item number, `t` the colour, and `g` is `male` or `female`. Only the
11 studio slots work here.

## Real-time tips

- Build the full outfit first, then turn on **Only the garment** to shoot each
  piece. The outfit stays as it is, so turning the switch off brings everything
  back.
- Jackets look best over a matching **Undershirt** and **Torsos**. Try a few
  combinations with the ped turned to ¾ before you take the picture.
- The stage keeps the last item while the next one loads. If a picture looks
  like the previous item, wait a moment for the new model to appear.
