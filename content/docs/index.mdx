clothingDB is a picture of every piece of clothing for the male and female ped,
each at an address that never changes. Use the pictures in a game UI, a website or anything else,
find items by number, or take your own pictures in the Creator Lab.

## At a glance

- One picture per item and colour, for male and female.
- No key, no account and no sign-up. An address loads anywhere.
- The item and colour numbers are the ones the game uses.
