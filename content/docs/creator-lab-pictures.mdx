---
title: Frame and save a picture
description: Only the garment, backdrops, angles, framing, picture size and file type, and what Capture view saves.
---

Once the ped is dressed, four settings decide what the picture looks like and
two decide the file you get.

## Only the garment

The switch at the top of the right column decides whether the body is in the
shot.

| Switch | The stage shows | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | The whole ped wearing the whole outfit | Character previews, outfit pictures |
| **On** | One piece, nothing else: no body, no other items | Shop items, inventory icons, item pictures |

When it is on, a row of buttons appears under it: **Which piece**. It lists the
slots the ped is wearing. Pick the one you want on its own, and the camera
frames that piece. Changing an item in the outfit list also points **Which
piece** at that slot, so the stage follows what you are working on.

If the chosen slot has nothing worn, the stage tells you so. Wear something in
that slot, or pick another piece.

<Callout>
  **Capture view** and **Batch capture** both follow this switch. Turn it on
  before a batch to get garment-only pictures like the ones on the CDN, or off to
  photograph the whole ped in each item.
</Callout>

## Backdrop

| Backdrop | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear** | A see-through background. The picture can sit on any colour |
| **Studio** | A solid dark grey background |
| **Green** | A solid green screen, for keying in another tool |
| **Magenta** | A solid magenta screen, for keying green clothes |

Pick **Clear** unless you need a solid background. With Clear, the checkerboard
on the stage shows where the picture will be see-through.

## Angle

Turns the camera around the ped: **Front**, **¾**, **Side** or **Back**. The
angle is exact, so every picture in a batch matches.

## Framing

How close the camera is, and on which part of the body:

| Framing | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Full** | Head to toe |
| **Upper** | Chest up to the head: tops, undershirts, accessories |
| **Lower** | Waist down: legs |
| **Feet** | Just the feet: shoes |
| **Head** | Just the head: masks, hair |

With **Only the garment** on, the camera frames the piece by itself, so framing
matters less. With it off, pick the framing that fits the slot you are showing.

## Output

| Setting | Choices | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size** | 256px, 512px, 1024px | Pictures are square. The CDN uses 512px |
| **Format** | WEBP, PNG | WebP files are much smaller. PNG opens in every program |

Both formats keep the see-through background.

## Capture view

**Capture view** at the top right saves exactly what the stage is set up to
show, at the size and format under Output. Your browser downloads it right away.

| Mode | File name |
| --- | --- |
| Only the garment on | `clothingDB-male-tops-245-0.webp` (ped, slot, item, colour) |
| Only the garment off | `clothingDB-male-1730103322000.webp` (ped and a timestamp) |

<Callout title="Your browser may ask first">
  The first time a site downloads files on its own, some browsers ask whether to
  allow it. Allow downloads for the site, or the picture is blocked.
</Callout>
