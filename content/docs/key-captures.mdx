---
title: Key captures
description: Remove the green or magenta background from your own in-game screenshots, number them and export them like the CDN.
---

**Key captures** is the second tab of the Creator Lab. It is for pictures you
took yourself in the game, for example of your server's own clothing, which the
Ped studio cannot load. It removes the solid background, frames the clothing,
names each picture and exports them in the same layout as the CDN.

Everything runs on your computer. Your screenshots are never uploaded.

## Take the screenshots in game

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Stand in front of a solid colour

    Use a plain **green** or **magenta** background that fills the whole
    picture behind the clothing. Use magenta for green clothes and green for
    everything else.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Light it evenly

    Avoid strong shadows on the background. The more even the colour, the
    cleaner the edges.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name the files (optional)

    If a file name contains the ped, slot, item and colour, the Lab reads them
    for you. Any of these work:

    ```txt
    male_tops_245_0.png
    female-shoes-12-3.png
    male tops 1 0.jpg
    ```

    The slot must be its name in the address, like `tops` or `hats`. See the
    [Slot reference](/docs/slots).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key them

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Drop the files in

    Open [/creator](/creator), switch to **Key captures**, and drop your
    screenshots on the page, or click to pick them. PNG, JPG and WebP work.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check each one

    Click a capture in the list to see the result. The **Preview backdrop**
    buttons show it on a checkerboard, black, white or the site's art, so you
    can spot leftover edges.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name it

    Under **This capture**, set the **Ped**, **Slot**, **Drawable** (item) and
    **Texture** (colour). A small picture shows what the CDN has under that name
    today, so you can check you numbered it right.

    Files without a readable name are numbered for you: each new one continues
    from the last, one colour higher.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Export

    **Download this one** saves the current capture. **Export as ZIP** saves all
    of them as `clothingDB-captures.zip`, laid out as `{ped}/{slot}/{item}/{colour}`
    like the CDN.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

These apply to every capture and update the previews as you change them.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Backdrop colour** | Auto | Which colour to remove. Auto detects green or magenta per picture |
| **Edge feather** | 2px | How soft the cut-out edge is. Raise it if edges look jagged |
| **Feather passes** | 2 | How many times the edge is softened. More passes, smoother edges |
| **Framing** | Fit garment | **Fit garment** crops to the clothing; **Centre crop** keeps the middle of the screenshot |
| **Padding** | 8% | Space around the clothing when framing is Fit garment |
| **Size** | 512px | The finished picture is square, 256, 512 or 1024 pixels |
| **Format** | WebP | WebP or PNG. Both keep the see-through background |

<Callout title="Green fringe on the edges?">
  Raise **Edge feather** a little, or switch **Backdrop colour** from Auto to the
  colour you used. If the clothing itself is green, retake it on magenta.
</Callout>
