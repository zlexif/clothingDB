The Creator Lab loads the real male and female ped from the game straight into
your browser. You dress it, turn it, frame it and save pictures, the same way a
photographer would in a studio. The game does not need to be open, and nothing
you make leaves your computer until you save it.

It has two tabs, switched at the top right:

- **Ped studio** dresses the ped and takes pictures of it. Most of these pages
  are about this tab.
- **Key captures** cleans up screenshots you already took in game on a green or
  magenta background. See [Key captures](/docs/key-captures).

<Callout title="Open it">
  Go to [/creator](/creator). The first time you visit, a short walkthrough
  points at each part of the screen. Press the **?** button next to the tabs to
  replay it any time.
</Callout>

## The screen at a glance

| Area | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ped** | Top of the left column | Switch between the male and female ped |
| **Your clothing** | Left column | Add your own clothing files and put them on the ped |
| **Outfit** | Left column | One row per clothing slot: wear it, take it off, pick the item and colour |
| **Stage** | Centre | The live ped. Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom |
| **Backdrop, Angle, Framing** | Under the stage | How the picture looks: background, camera direction, and how close |
| **Only the garment** | Top of the right column | Hide the body and every other item, and frame one piece on its own |
| **Output** | Right column | Picture size and file type |
| **Batch capture** | Right column | Photograph a whole range of items in every colour, saved as one ZIP |
| **Capture view** | Top right | Save exactly what the stage shows, right now |

## Your first picture

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Wait for the ped

    The stage says **Loading the ped…** for a moment, then the male ped appears
    in a plain outfit: a top, legs and shoes, all item 0.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Change the top

    In the **Tops / Jackets** row, press the **+** on the left counter a few
    times. Each press puts the next top on the ped. The right counter changes its
    colour.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Keep only the top

    Turn on **Only the garment** in the right column. The body and the other
    items disappear and the camera moves in on the top.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save it

    Leave the backdrop on **Clear** for a see-through background and press
    **Capture view**. Your browser downloads a picture named like
    `clothingDB-male-tops-12-0.webp`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Creator Lab can and cannot do

- It covers the 11 clothing slots worn on the body: tops, undershirts, torsos,
  legs, shoes, masks, hair, accessories, armor, bags and decals.
- Props (hats, glasses, watches and the rest) and face overlays (makeup, facial
  hair and the rest) are not in the studio. Their pictures are still on the CDN
  and in the [Directory](/browse).
- Besides the game's own clothing, you can add your own clothing files (.ydd
  and .ytd) and photograph them. Escrowed (encrypted) clothing cannot be added,
  because it only opens inside FiveM. See [Your own clothing](/docs/creator-lab-custom).
- It works best on a computer. On a phone the stage is small, and big batches
  are slow.
