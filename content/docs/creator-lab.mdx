---
title: Creator Lab
description: Dress the real ped in your browser and save see-through pictures.
---

The Creator Lab loads the real male and female ped in your browser. The game
does not need to be open. It has two tabs: **Ped studio** and **Key captures**.

## Ped studio

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Dress the ped

    Pick male or female, then choose an item and colour number for each slot in
    the outfit list. The numbers are the same ones the Directory shows.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set up the shot

    Choose a backdrop (Clear gives a see-through background), an angle and a
    framing. Turn on **Only the garment** to hide the body and every other item
    and keep one piece on its own, or leave it off to photograph the whole ped.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save it

    **Capture view** saves exactly what you see, at the size and format you
    picked under Output.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Coming from the Directory?">
  Every item in the Directory has a **Try it on** link that opens the Ped studio
  already wearing it.
</Callout>

## Photographing a whole range

Under **Batch capture**, pick a slot and a range of item numbers. The studio
photographs every item in every colour, each framed on the garment, and gives
you one ZIP. The **Only the garment** switch decides whether the body is in
those shots too.

Files in the ZIP are sorted like the picture addresses:

```txt
male/tops/245/0.webp
male/tops/245/1.webp
…
```

## Key captures

Already have screenshots from the game on a green or magenta background? Drop
them in and the background is removed for you. Nothing you add leaves your
browser.
